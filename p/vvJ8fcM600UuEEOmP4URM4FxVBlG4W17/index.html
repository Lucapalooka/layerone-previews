Good to know

Preparing for a first consultation

Clear documents and a short summary make the first meeting count.

Write a short timeline

A one-page summary of what happened, with dates, helps an attorney assess your matter quickly.

Gather documents

Contracts, letters, notices, invoices and identity documents relevant to the matter.

Ask about fees

Ask how fees are charged and whether an estimate or fee agreement can be provided in writing.

Confidentiality

Discussions with an attorney about your matter are confidential; ask how your documents will be handled.

Have this ready when you get in touch
  • A short summary of the matter
  • Relevant documents and correspondence
  • Names of other parties involved
  • Any deadlines or court dates
